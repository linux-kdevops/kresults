# Format versions

The versions of the format, newest first. A change to the format,
including a change of meaning that leaves the fields as they are, raises
the version by one and adds its entry here: what was added, changed and
removed, and what a reader of the version before must do.

## 1

The first version ([README.md](README.md)). A record says what tools
reported on a subject, whatever producer ran them. It holds one entry for
each distinct run of a command, with the invocations of who ran it.
